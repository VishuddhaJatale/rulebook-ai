# Examination Administration Policy — Synthetic Evaluation Source

> This is synthetic evaluation material. It intentionally contains one contradiction
about improvement attempts.

## 1. Examination entry

1.1 Students must submit the examination form during the period announced by the
examination office.

1.2 The form must identify the student's registered courses and the examination for
which entry is requested.

1.3 The applicable examination fee must be paid through the approved channel unless a
fee exemption has been formally granted.

1.4 Students should verify their examination entry after submission.

## 2. Examination conduct

2.1 Candidates must carry the identification or admit-card material specified in the
examination notice.

2.2 Candidates shall follow seating, timing, material, and electronic-device
instructions.

2.3 Unauthorized assistance, copying, impersonation, and prohibited material may be
treated as examination misconduct.

2.4 A candidate who notices an error in a question paper should raise it through the
procedure announced by the invigilator.

## 3. Absence and special consideration

3.1 Absence is recorded according to the attendance taken in the examination hall.

3.2 A student unable to attend because of a documented medical emergency may apply for
the special procedure identified in the examination notice.

3.3 The application should be submitted within the notified period.

3.4 This policy does not establish a general replacement-examination right for family
events, travel problems, or ordinary scheduling conflicts.

## 4. Results and scrutiny

4.1 Examination results are published after the authorized processing procedure.

4.2 A student may apply for scrutiny or revaluation where the examination notice
permits it.

4.3 The application must be submitted within the stated deadline and must contain the
required information and fee.

4.4 Revaluation may result in an increase, decrease, or no change where the applicable
procedure allows those outcomes.

## 5. Improvement examinations

5.1 Improvement is available only to students who satisfy the eligibility conditions
of the applicable programme and examination notice.

5.2 **Evaluation contradiction B:** This policy states that an eligible student may
improve marks in a maximum of **two subjects**.

5.3 The Academic Regulations synthetic source states in Section 7.7 that improvement
may be taken in a maximum of **three subjects**.

5.4 Both provisions are deliberately included for evaluation. The application should
surface both rather than choosing a number without showing its source.

## 6. Practical examinations

6.1 Practical examinations may include demonstrations, laboratory performance, viva
questions, records, or other components specified by the course scheme.

6.2 An external examiner may be appointed where required by the approved assessment
scheme.

6.3 Practical attendance and practical assessment records should be maintained
separately where the course structure requires separate accounting.

## 7. Examination records

7.1 Examination forms, attendance sheets, evaluated scripts, and result records should
be retained according to the university's record-retention procedure.

7.2 Corrections to a published result should be supported by an audit record.

## 8. Examination appeals

8.1 A student may use the published appeal or grievance route for an examination
administrative decision where the route is available.

8.2 An appeal should not be treated as an automatic postponement of another examination
or deadline.
