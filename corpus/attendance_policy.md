# Attendance Administration Policy — Synthetic Evaluation Source

> This file is synthetic project material. It intentionally contains one of the
> three contradictions used by the evaluation.

## 1. General attendance requirement

1.1 Regular students are expected to attend the lectures and practical classes
prescribed for each registered course. Attendance is maintained independently for each
course unless the approved scheme states otherwise.

1.2 The ordinary minimum attendance requirement is 75 percent. A student below the
ordinary threshold is not automatically eligible merely because the difference is
small.

1.3 Attendance is calculated from delivered sessions. A cancelled session that is not
subsequently conducted is not counted as an absence.

1.4 Authorized university activities may receive attendance treatment only where the
student has documentary authorization from the competent authority.

## 2. Attendance records

2.1 Departments should update attendance records regularly and provide students with
reasonable opportunities to identify apparent errors.

2.2 A correction request should include the date, course, session, and reason the
record is believed to be incorrect.

2.3 The department may consult class registers, laboratory logs, approved activity
lists, and faculty records before deciding a correction request.

2.4 Attendance records should not be altered merely on the basis of a student's oral
statement.

## 3. Condonation process

3.1 A request for condonation must be filed through the notified process.

3.2 The request should specify the period of absence and the reason for the absence.

3.3 Supporting records may include medical certificates, official activity
authorizations, or other documents accepted by the competent authority.

3.4 Approval is discretionary within the limits of the applicable regulation. Filing an
application does not itself change the attendance percentage.

3.5 A student remains responsible for meeting the ordinary attendance requirement unless
and until an approved condonation changes the student's eligibility status.

## 4. Medical absence

4.1 Medical absence should be supported by a certificate or other evidence accepted by
the university.

4.2 The university may ask a student to submit the record for verification when the
absence is substantial or when the issuing provider cannot be verified.

4.3 Medical evidence does not automatically guarantee exemption from an examination or
attendance requirement. The competent authority must apply the applicable rule.

4.4 **Evaluation contradiction A:** For an approved medical exemption from the ordinary
attendance requirement, this policy states that the student's attendance must be at
least **65 percent**.

4.5 The medical threshold in Section 4.4 is intentionally inconsistent with Section
4.8 of the synthetic Academic Regulations file, which states 60 percent. Neither
document should be silently preferred by the retrieval system when both provisions are
applicable.

## 5. Other absence categories

5.1 Participation in an authorized university competition, official academic visit,
or formally assigned university activity may be considered under the relevant
attendance procedure.

5.2 Personal travel, routine family functions, private appointments, and ordinary
social events are not guaranteed grounds for attendance relief.

5.3 Students should not rely on informal assurances that an absence will be excused.

## 6. Examination eligibility

6.1 Attendance eligibility is assessed using the records applicable to the relevant
examination.

6.2 Where an approved condonation is granted, the decision should be recorded before
final examination eligibility is published where practicable.

6.3 A student whose request remains undecided should contact the designated academic
office rather than assume approval.

## 7. Appeals

7.1 A student may challenge an attendance record correction decision through the
academic review process where such a route is available.

7.2 An appeal should identify the disputed attendance entries and attach relevant
evidence.

7.3 The appeal authority may uphold, modify, or return the decision for reconsideration.

## 8. Interpretation

8.1 Attendance provisions must be read according to their stated scope.

8.2 Where the corpus contains incompatible medical thresholds for the same situation,
the application must return a contradiction state and display the evidence.

8.3 The application must not infer a medical exemption threshold from practices at
other universities.
