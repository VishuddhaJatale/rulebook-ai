# Semester Fee Schedule — Synthetic Evaluation Source

> This is a synthetic fee schedule used to test table extraction and contradiction
handling. It is not an official university fee notice.

| Fee item | Normal deadline | Late-payment window | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Semester tuition fee | 15 July | 16–25 July | Late fee applies during the late window |
| Examination fee | 10 October | 11–15 October | Applicable to the following examination cycle |
| Hostel fee | 20 July | 21–30 July | Subject to room-allocation confirmation |
| Project registration fee | 5 August | 6–10 August | Final-year students only |

## 1. Payment process

1.1 Fees must be paid through the payment channel identified by the university.

1.2 A transaction is considered successful only when the payment provider confirms the
transaction and the university system records the payment.

1.3 Students should retain the transaction receipt until the end of the academic year.

## 2. Semester tuition deadline

2.1 The table above lists **15 July** as the normal semester tuition deadline.

2.2 **Evaluation contradiction C:** Section 3.1 below states that semester tuition is
due by **31 July**. This conflict is intentional. A question asking for the final
normal deadline must therefore be classified as contradictory if both provisions are
retrieved as applicable.

## 3. Alternative deadline statement

3.1 For the purposes of this synthetic evaluation source, the semester tuition fee is
stated to be due by **31 July**.

3.2 This statement intentionally conflicts with the fee table in Section 2.1.

## 4. Late payment

4.1 Payment after the applicable normal deadline may incur the late charge stated in
the fee notice.

4.2 A student should not assume that a late-payment window extends eligibility for
course registration unless the relevant registration notice says so.

4.3 A payment made after a late window may require administrative approval or may be
rejected, depending on the applicable notice.

## 5. Refunds

5.1 Refund eligibility depends on the type of fee and the circumstances of withdrawal.

5.2 This synthetic schedule does not specify the refund percentage for withdrawal from
a semester after classes have begun.

## 6. Payment disputes

6.1 A student who is charged but whose payment is not reflected in the university
portal should preserve the bank or payment-provider transaction reference.

6.2 The finance office may request transaction evidence before correcting a payment
record.
